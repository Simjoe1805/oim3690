## what I learned this week 
- learned the evolution of CSS Layout. 
- includes tables in the 90s, floats in the 2000s, flexbox in the 2010s, and 2017 onwards the Grid. 
- explains how flexbox is a one direction at a time while grid is two directions at once. it is also explained that it is usually used both on the same page 
- Flexbox is the easiest way to center anything. 
- the specific usage of when to use flexbox and when to use grid. 
- use flexbox when content flows in one direction, and you want items to determine their own size, while you use grid when you need rows and columns and want precise control over the layout.
